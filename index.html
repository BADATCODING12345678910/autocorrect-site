What you get

As-you-type autocorrect

High-confidence typo fixes system-wide, including mid-word AHK-style rules, with Undo toast and per-app profiles.

Win+H dictation cleanup

After Windows Voice Typing, auto-Fix cleans speech mistakes — skips if you moved focus, so it never hijacks another window.

Under-word Fix card

Suggestions park under the mistake with a Why line and confidence chip. Accept, Skip, Never, or Undo.

Rewrite + tone packs

Highlight text, then Email, Slack, or Formal reply — or type your own instruction. Uses your OpenAI key.

Snippets

Type ;sig (or your own triggers) and expand signatures and boilerplate without mixing them into typo autocorrect.

Clipboard Fix

Win+Alt+C cleans whatever is on the clipboard. Optional paste-after for a one-chord cleanup.

Word Manager

Add custom pairs, bulk Never, search learned vs never lists, and import/export — your dictionary, your rules.

Themes & updates

Appearance themes for tray and UI. Check for updates downloads with a progress bar and retries — no reinstall for normal upgrades.

Languages on demand

Pick a grammar language (or use Auto-detect) and the matching as-you-type pack downloads only when needed. American English stays bundled.

Also built in: Pause hotkey, protect zones (URLs, emails, code), AutoLearn from your corrections, LanguageTool + optional ChatGPT writing suggestions (bring your own key), privacy mode, Accept-all, and themes with in-app updates.
